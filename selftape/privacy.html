Self·Tape privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Self·Tape is made by Layernest Ltd. This policy covers the Self·Tape iPhone app.

In short: Self·Tape does not collect your personal data. There is no account, analytics, advertising or tracking, and no server of ours receives your data.

What stays on your iPhone

Your auditions, scripts, casting emails, takes, tapes and settings are kept in the app on your iPhone. They go into your iPhone’s backups the way Apple handles app data, except the reader voice files, which are left out. We never receive a copy.

Camera, microphone, speech recognition and photos

The app uses the front camera and microphone to record your takes. It uses speech recognition to keep the script at your pace. It opens Photos only for a photo of a script you choose, or to save a tape when you tap Download. Speech recognition, and the reading of your scripts and casting emails, run on your iPhone with Apple’s on-device frameworks, including Apple Intelligence where it’s available. None of it is sent to us.

The first time, speech recognition may need Apple’s speech download for your language. The app asks first; when you tap Download, iOS fetches it from Apple, and nothing of yours is sent.

Reader voices

When you tap Download, the app fetches the voice files from Hugging Face and jsDelivr. The requests carry nothing of yours. As with any download, those services see your network address, under their own privacy policies.

Sharing

Your files leave the app only when you share, send or save them, to a place you choose.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought and managed through Apple. We don’t receive your payment details. The app checks your subscription with Apple on your iPhone.

Keeping and deleting

Your data stays until you delete it. Deleting an audition removes its scripts, takes and tapes from the app, and deleting the app removes everything it holds. We hold no copy, so there’s nothing for us to delete or hand over.

Changes

A new version of this policy will be posted here with a new date.
