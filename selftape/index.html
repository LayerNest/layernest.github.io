Self·Tape

Self-tapes with a reader. For iPhone.

Self·Tape helps actors film audition self-tapes on their own: a reader for the other parts, the script by the lens, and tapes named the way casting asks.

For questions or problems, email david@layernest-ltd.com.

Help

  • Pro on a new iPhone: open Plans from Settings → PLAN and tap Restore purchases.
  • Cancel Pro: in your Apple Account settings, under Subscriptions.
  • Which iPhones: Self·Tape works on iPhone 11 and later with iOS 26. Reading casting’s email for you needs Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later); on other iPhones you type in what casting asked for.
